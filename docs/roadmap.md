## Topic Recommendation Logic

Topic recommendations in the weekly sheet are generated by:
1. Surfacing topics not yet started (hoursLogged == 0)
2. Flagging topics under 50% of their target hours
3. Suggesting a different track if one track dominates >60% of weekly hours
