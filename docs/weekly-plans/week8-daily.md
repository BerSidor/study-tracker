# Week 8 — Day-by-Day: Startup Thesis + Fall Launch Plan

> **Week goal:** exit summer with a committed, validated idea and a concrete Fall execution plan.
> **Ship by Saturday:** a thesis one-pager + MVP scope + Fall roadmap. **The summer goal, achieved.**
> **Study days:** Mon–Sat (Sunday rest). ~6.7 h/day. Log every block with the tracker.

Everything converges here. You have the skills (Wks 1–5), the market evidence (Wks 6–7), and a
chosen niche. This week you write it down sharply enough to execute — and to pitch.

---

## Day 1 (Mon) — Draft the Thesis One-Pager `~6h`
**Tracker:** `start "Startup thesis & MVP scope"`

- **Write:** problem, customer (be specific — a person, not "businesses"), and your solution in
  plain language. Anchor each claim to evidence from your customer conversations.

## Day 2 (Tue) — Why-Claude-Code, Why-Now, Defensibility `~6h`
**Tracker:** `start "Startup thesis & MVP scope"`

- **Sharpen:** why is Claude Code the right engine for this? Why is now the moment? What stops a
  competitor copying you in a weekend (data, distribution, integrations, domain depth)?

## Day 3 (Wed) — Business Model + Pricing `~6h`
**Tracker:** `start "Startup thesis & MVP scope"`

- **Model:** pricing, unit economics (use real Week-4 token costs), and a rough path to
  break-even. What does the first $1k/month look like?

## Day 4 (Thu) — Detailed MVP Scope `~7h`
**Tracker:** `start "Startup thesis & MVP scope"`

- **Scope:** the buildable MVP — core workflow, the MCP/Agent-SDK pieces you'll reuse from the
  summer, what's explicitly *out* of v1. Estimate build time honestly.

## Day 5 (Fri) — Fall Roadmap `~7h`
**Tracker:** `start "Startup thesis & MVP scope"`

- **Plan:** week-by-week Fall build order. Define your path to the **first 5 customers** (likely
  your warm gig/interview contacts). Set one measurable milestone for end of Fall.

## Day 6 (Sat) — Polish + Summer Retrospective `~6h`
**Tracker:** `start "Startup thesis & MVP scope"`

- **Finalize:** tighten the one-pager so a stranger gets it in 60 seconds.
- **Retrospective:** review the whole summer in the tracker sheet — hours by track, what shipped,
  what you'd do differently. **Run `report`** one last time.

---

## Week 8 Definition of Done
- [ ] A one-page startup thesis a stranger understands in 60 seconds
- [ ] A detailed, buildable MVP scope with an honest time estimate
- [ ] A Fall roadmap with a path to the first 5 customers + one end-of-Fall milestone
- [ ] Summer retrospective written
- [ ] All study blocks logged — **the summer goal is complete**
