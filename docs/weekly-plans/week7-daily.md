# Week 7 — Day-by-Day: Validation

> **Week goal:** pressure-test your top 1–2 niches with real people, and narrow to one.
> **Ship by Saturday:** one committed thesis direction backed by customer conversations + an MVP scope draft.
> **Study days:** Mon–Sat (Sunday rest). ~6.7 h/day. Log every block with the tracker.

Ideas die or harden when they meet real customers. Your gig clients are warm leads — use them.
The goal is conviction: by Saturday you commit to *one* direction.

---

## Day 1 (Mon) — Line Up Conversations `~6h`
**Tracker:** `start "Customer conversations"`

- **Prep (3h):** write a short interview script for each candidate niche — focus on *their* pain,
  not your idea. Avoid leading questions.
- **Outreach (3h):** reach out to 8–10 potential customers (gig clients first — they already
  trust you), aim to book 4–6 conversations.

## Day 2 (Tue) — Conversations, Round 1 `~7h`
**Tracker:** `start "Customer conversations"`

- **Talk + log:** run conversations. Listen for: how painful is this *really*? What do they do
  today? Would they pay? Capture verbatim quotes in the Discovery Log.

## Day 3 (Wed) — Synthesize & Narrow `~6h`
**Tracker:** `start "Customer conversations"`

- **Analyze:** which niche got the strongest "yes, this hurts, I'd pay" signal? Narrow your top 2
  to a leading candidate. Note what would have to be true for it to work.

## Day 4 (Thu) — MVP Scoping with Claude Code `~7h`
**Tracker:** `start "MVP scoping with Claude Code"`

- **Scope:** define the smallest thing that delivers real value for the leading niche. What's
  the one core workflow? What can Claude Code / an MCP / the Agent SDK do most of? Cut ruthlessly.

## Day 5 (Fri) — Conversations Round 2 + Gig Wrap `~7h`
**Tracker:** `start "Customer conversations"` → `start "Client gigs & delivery"`

- **Validate (4h):** show the MVP scope (mockup/description) to 2–3 people. Does it land?
- **Gigs (3h):** wrap up outstanding gigs cleanly — keep these clients as future references.

## Day 6 (Sat) — Commit to One Thesis `~6h`
**Tracker:** `start "MVP scoping with Claude Code"`

- **Decide:** commit to ONE direction. Write the one-paragraph thesis + the evidence behind it.
- **Discovery Log:** archive the validation findings. **Run `report`.**

---

## Week 7 Definition of Done
- [ ] 4–6 real customer conversations completed and logged
- [ ] Narrowed from 2 candidates to 1 committed direction
- [ ] An MVP scope draft for the chosen niche
- [ ] MVP scope shown to 2–3 people for reaction
- [ ] All study blocks logged; gigs wrapped cleanly
