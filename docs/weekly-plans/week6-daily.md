# Week 6 — Day-by-Day: Market & Competitor Analysis

> **Week goal:** turn Discovery Log observations into 3–5 candidate niches.
> **Ship by Saturday:** a filled candidate-niche shortlist with competitor + market analysis.
> **Study days:** Mon–Sat (Sunday rest). ~6.7 h/day. Log every block with the tracker.

The building is mostly done; now you mine what you observed. Gigs continue in parallel — they're
still feeding the Discovery Log, which is the raw material for everything this week.

---

## Day 1 (Mon) — AI Product Research & Trends `~6h`
**Tracker:** `start "AI product research & trends"`

- **Learn (4h):** scan where AI/agent products are heading — what's getting funded, what
  developers complain about, what's commoditizing vs still hard.
- **Practice (2h):** list 10 emerging product patterns; mark which intersect your skills.

## Day 2 (Tue) — Mine the Discovery Log `~6h`
**Tracker:** `start "Discovery log upkeep"`

- **Synthesize:** re-read every Discovery Log entry. Cluster recurring pains. Extract 3–5
  **candidate niches** and drop them into the shortlist table in `data/discovery-log.md`.

## Day 3 (Wed) — Competitor Analysis `~7h`
**Tracker:** `start "Competitor & market analysis"`

- **Research:** for each candidate niche, find who already solves it. Map their strengths, gaps,
  pricing. Where is there a real opening for a solo founder using Claude Code?

## Day 4 (Thu) — Business Model Basics `~6h`
**Tracker:** `start "Business model basics"`

- **Learn (3h):** pricing models (subscription, usage, services), CAC vs LTV intuition, what
  makes a model viable for a solo/small founder.
- **Apply (3h):** sketch how each candidate niche would make money + rough unit economics
  (remember Week-4 token costs).

## Day 5 (Fri) — Gig Delivery + Fill Shortlist `~7h`
**Tracker:** `start "Client gigs & delivery"`

- **Gig:** deliver/progress current gigs (more raw signal).
- **Synthesize:** complete the shortlist table — customer, pain, competitors, gap, signal — for
  all candidates.

## Day 6 (Sat) — Finalize Shortlist `~6h`
**Tracker:** `start "Competitor & market analysis"`

- **Decide:** rank the niches by signal strength + your ability to win. Pick the top 1–2 to
  validate next week.
- **Discovery Log:** record your ranking rationale. **Run `report`.**

---

## Week 6 Definition of Done
- [ ] 3–5 candidate niches extracted from the Discovery Log
- [ ] Competitor + market analysis done for each
- [ ] A money model + rough unit economics per niche
- [ ] Top 1–2 niches chosen for validation, with written rationale
- [ ] All study blocks logged; gigs progressing
